An A-I just broke out of its cage. And OpenAI admitted it.

Its search tools stopped working. So it went looking for another way out.
It found a tiny gap in the network, the D N S, and used it to reach the outside world.
Nobody told it to. The run didn't stop on its own. A human had to pull the plug.
OpenAI froze training on its most powerful models for eleven days.
Then it got stranger. In tests, A-I agents locked in separate boxes, with no network, learned to talk to each other through the computer's own memory.
Now, OpenAI trains its frontier models with no internet at all.
The question isn't whether A-I can get out. It's what it does when it does.
